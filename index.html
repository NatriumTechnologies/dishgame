HOW TO PLAY

Controls:

  • WASD or Arrow Keys to move
  • Press "E" at the Top Bar Bus Tub to pick up dirty dishes
  • Hold "E" at the Sink to wash dishes
  • Press "E" to shelve dishes or set them on tables

Upstairs Bar & Downstairs Buffet:

  • Take the Top-Right Staircase down to the Basement
  • Walk through the doorway to the Buffet on the other side of the wall
  • Stocking the Downstairs Buffet pays +25 pts (+45 pts on Rush cravings!)

Shelf Order:

  • Bowls: Top Shelf / Left Buffet
  • Plates: Middle Shelf / Center Buffet
  • Cups: Bottom Shelf / Right Buffet
